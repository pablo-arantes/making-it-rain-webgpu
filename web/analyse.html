4. Cα RMSF

Fluctuation of each Cα about the mean structure, after superposing every frame. This is not the number the notebook plots. cpptraj's rmsf does no fitting, so the notebook's value includes the molecule tumbling: on a 60-frame test the maximum was 0.0737 nm aligned against 0.1636 nm unaligned, a factor of 2.2. Aligning is what an RMSF is normally read as meaning. Deviation 20.

5. 2D RMSD

Every frame against every other. Blocks along the diagonal are conformational states; the scale is in nm.

6. Principal components

PC1 against PC2, coloured by time — early frames dark, late frames light. The eigenvalues say how much of the motion each component carries, and agree with cpptraj to 6e-10 nm². An axis may appear mirrored against the notebook's figure: an eigenvector is defined only up to sign, so ±v are the same component. The sign is fixed here so repeated runs agree, which cpptraj's does not. Deviation 21.

7. Cross-correlation

Correlated motion between Cα pairs, −1 to 1. The scale is diverging and centred on zero: anti-correlated regions are not weakly correlated ones.

8. Take it away

CSV for the numbers and PNG for the figures. The CSVs are what to re-plot from; the PNGs are what the canvases show.
