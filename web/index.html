Making it Rain on WebGPU

Molecular dynamics in this tab, on your own GPU. Nothing is uploaded.

Experimental. The engine runs: forces, constraints, integrators, thermostat, barostat, restraints and minimiser, each held to OpenMM. This page reports your GPU and runs the precision checks the design depends on.

Prepare a system → a PDB ID or your own structure becomes an OpenMM System: hydrogens, a water box, ions.

Run a system → already have a System and State? Check them against the double-precision reference and run.

Analyse a trajectory → Cα RMSD, radius of gyration, RMSF, 2D RMSD, principal components and cross-correlation, from any .dcd.

WebGPU has no f64 and no 64-bit atomics. The engine therefore accumulates forces in 64-bit fixed point built from 32-bit atomics, and keeps positions in two f32 words. These checks measure both on your device against an independent reference. Why.
